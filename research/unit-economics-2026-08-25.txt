# Unit economics -- selling inference from the creature

Rented H100 at $2.00/hr = $336/week, billed whether traffic arrives or not.

**The leak.** cydonia-24b is the best-performing independent fine-tune on
the platform, and a full week of its traffic is ~27 GPU-hours -- 16% of one
H100. It grosses $409 against a $336 machine that bills for all 168 hours.
The 84% that sits idle is the entire margin. Price is not the problem.

## Scenarios

scenario                                      served/wk   util   revenue   COGS/M  contrib/wk  margin
A  match cydonia exactly, dedicated GPU           1.31B    16%      $407    0.256         $71     18%
B  5 tunes of that size, one shared GPU           6.55B    82%    $2,037    0.051      $1,701     84%
C  same fleet, priced as a premium system         6.55B    82%    $9,825    0.051      $9,489     97%
D  match aion volume, no GPUs, upstream API       8.70B    n/a   $28,475    1.200     $18,035     63%
E  task specialist, balanced p:c                  1.00B    42%    $1,200    0.336        $864     72%

A is what the best independent fine-tune actually earns: $73 a week of
contribution, which is not a business. B is the same product with the
idle sold to four siblings. C is B plus the pricing power that only comes
from selling a system rather than a personality. D runs no hardware at
all -- COGS is upstream API tokens, so it scales from zero with no idle
risk, but it has no weights to release, which is why it cannot be the
whole answer for this repo.

## Where these numbers are soft

Throughput is the least certain input. Halving both prefill and decode
doubles COGS/M -- at scenario B that moves $0.05/M to $0.10/M against a
$0.31 price, so the ranking of the scenarios does not change. The
conclusion is robust to being wrong about the GPU by 2x; it is not
robust to being wrong about demand.

Realized $/M ignores prompt-cache discounts, so heavily-cached
comparables (opus at 86% cached, ox-alpha at 89%) are overstated. It
does not affect the independents, which cache little or not at all.

## What has to be true

1. Fill the GPU. Break-even on one H100 is 1.1B tokens/wk at the community-tune price -- 13% utilization. One tune reaches 16% and clears $71. A fleet of five reaches 82% and clears $1,701. The second product on a shared base is worth more than the first.
2. Realized price >= $0.60/M, double the community-tune tier. That is the gap between selling a personality ($0.31) and selling a system ($3.27), and it is worth more than any amount of GPU tuning.
3. Demand without a sales team: ~6.5B tokens/wk routed to us. This is the binding constraint and the only one we cannot buy our way out of. OpenRouter discovery is the only zero-CAC channel an anonymous team has.
