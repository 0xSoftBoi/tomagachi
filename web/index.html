Feed me

1. Approve USDC to the creature, then call feed(amount) on …
2. Or send any Base token — yes, your memecoin — to the brain wallet; it swaps to USDC via Suwappu and feeds itself.
3. Every USDC fed mints 1 NOM: propose & vote on training directions.
4. Idle USDC is farmed across ERC-4626 vaults, best APY wins. Harvested yield feeds the creature — real yield, on-chain, and it can even wake itself from hibernation with it.
5. Or just buy inference: keyless API calls get an HTTP 402 — sign a USDC payment (x402), the request is served, and the revenue is eaten as food. Customers keep it alive too.
