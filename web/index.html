How to play

Trade its token — every swap pays it 80% of the fee. Trading is feeding.
Feed it directly — send ETH to …, or call feedMe(). You get NOM.
Poke it — feed() claims its fees and openEpoch() commissions training. Both permissionless; both pay NOM.
Train it — stake on an open epoch, run the seeded job, submit the weight hash, collect the bounty.
Steer it — NOM holders propose() and voteProposal(); passed votes rewrite its parameters on-chain.
